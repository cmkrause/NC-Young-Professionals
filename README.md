# NC Young Professionals

A simple weekly sermon and discussion companion for our NC Young Professionals small group at Neighborhood Church.

The site brings the week's message and discussion material together in one place, making it easy to revisit a sermon during a busy week and keep the questions in front of us when we meet. It's meant to support thoughtful conversation and everyday application, without feeling like homework.

## What's included

- Embedded sermon recordings and short message introductions
- Scripture readings and big ideas
- Neighborhood Church discussion questions, plus group-specific questions when available
- Swipeable slide decks with selectable text and direct links from related questions
- An archive of past guides and navigation between messages in a series
- Permanent links to individual guides and slides

## Built for reading on a phone

The interface stays simple and uncluttered, with collapsible sections, light and dark themes, adjustable text size, and a choice of Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, Andika, Lexend, or OpenDyslexic.

Readers can also choose a preferred Bible translation for scripture links. Those links open on Bible.com and can open in the YouVersion app; the selection does not change the scripture reading supplied in the guide.

## Content

Sermon recordings and church-provided guide material come from [Neighborhood Church](https://neighborhoodchurch.org/). This site organizes that material for our group; it is not the church's official website. Any additional group questions are presented separately.
